## Purpose and source selection

The eligible-product audit found mostly shared order/document questions. Nine eligible pages subsequently received specific literature context. The eight below still lacked it. This release adds brief, attributed research descriptions to those existing pages, without changing specifications, inventory or original lab records. The live NAD+ page was independently checked before editing and contained the existing questions but no research section.

Sources are primary papers selected for exact named material and a bounded experimental question. They are not a comprehensive review, an endorsement, proof of efficacy or evidence that BIOMOD supplied the authors' materials. New copy states the material/model and endpoint and links the original study. All context retains the existing warning that literature does not verify a BIOMOD lot or suitability for human use.

## Catalog and document boundaries

The current catalog explicitly supports the selected named molecules. Semax, Selank, Tesamorelin and NAD+ retain their listed/certificate lot mismatches. KPV retains pending documentation. AOD, Kisspeptin and PT-141 retain their own matching-lot records. Literature cannot close these document gaps. Original PDFs, lot records, prices, stock, artwork, site structure and publication holds are unchanged. Eight additions bring literature coverage to all 17 currently eligible pages; that is content coverage, not Google indexing or ranking coverage.

Publication years follow journal issues: AOD 2015 despite 2014 online publication, KPV 2008 despite 2007 online publication, Tesamorelin 2007 despite 2006 online publication, NAD 2018 despite 2017 online publication. No new source quotations or borrowed figures are used.
