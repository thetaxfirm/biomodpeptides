# Reply flow and lot matching, October 28, 2026

Owner request: make the employee request desk easy to use, prepare useful Kim C. drafts from purchase and email history, show whether a real COA PDF is attached, and accept lot numbers entered without spaces or dashes. No customer messages are sent by this release or its verification.

The employee composer now leads with the customer request, attachment result, visible Create AI draft control, editable email and direct Send. Purchase-count narratives and audit-method explanations are removed from the main workflow. Optional order follow-ups retain a concise payment warning when relevant. Compact records remain available under internal details; evidence still informs drafting.

An authenticated read-only attachment check verifies the current requested lot, selected original PDF and its stored hash before reporting that a PDF is ready. It does not create a draft, approve a send or return document bytes. Responses are bound to the current request, revision and attachment choice; late responses cannot validate a new selection. Failed or unmatched automatic selection has an explicit Reply without a PDF option. Cancelled/sent drafts clear old selections. A public COA link is explicitly separate from an attached PDF.

Lot comparison ignores formatting separators while preserving all letters, digits and leading zeroes. For example, 102300001, 102 30 0001 and 102-30-0001 match. Added/missing digits, letter substitutions and malformed input do not. Multiple reports with the same normalized lot require review unless one authoritative administrator-approved association resolves the choice. Original report bytes and stored lot text remain unchanged. The rule is shared across public lot requests, automatic lookup, staff attachment preview and reply validation.

The local Jarvis companion reads bounded direct customer correspondence only from existing TryBiomod business mailboxes for draft context. It excludes unrelated/personal/group mail content, identifies unavailable coverage, and keeps source text out of persistent review notes. The writer uses verified purchase and prior-contact facts and avoids repeating a discount or claiming payment failed without evidence. Kim C. remains the customer-care identity. Routine drafts remain unsent until website Send; genuine owner-decision cases retain their approval requirement.

Existing reply rows and historical inquiries are not automatically replayed. The retired direct COA queue stays disabled. If a queued draft's attachment evidence materially changes, the bridge requires a fresh review instead of silently adding a newly eligible report. Purchase snapshots, prices, stock, runtime overrides, account permissions, credentials and original COAs are preserved.

Affected consumers: /admin?tab=requests, /admin?tab=today, public COA lot-request validation, original-PDF selection and the local Jarvis reply bridge. Isolated regressions cover request revisions, PDF failures, attachment races, ambiguous/malformed lots, plain replies, follow-up waiting counts and actual correspondence filtering.
