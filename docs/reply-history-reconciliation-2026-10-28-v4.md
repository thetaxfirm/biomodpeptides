# Reply history reconciliation, October 28, 2026

The owner reported already-sent replies appearing as unanswered. Conversation tracking previously imported incoming messages but could miss later answers sent directly from the verified mailbox. This release imports both directions for the exact linked provider conversation, preserves the original sender, closes questions answered by a later verified message, and invalidates stale unsent drafts and approvals. Repeated imports do not invalidate newer work. Equal timestamps, new questions and in-flight sends remain protected.

Changed email subjects are associated only when an owned order, exact provider thread/account/recipient and the full normalized customer inquiry agree with an existing verified sent answer. An order number or customer email alone cannot close another question. Local records marked sent without a provider receipt become Check sent status and cannot be prepared or sent again. The receipt hold survives later incoming messages and outside-history reconciliation.

Affected surfaces: /admin?tab=requests, /admin?tab=today, request draft/approval/send validation, external mailbox reconciliation and the existing local Jarvis bridge. Historical messages, purchase records, original COAs, pricing, stock, credentials and roles are preserved. No new customer emails, payment checks or test orders are sent. The stopped scheduled importer is not run.

Verification: isolated component and SQLite regressions cover duplicate imports, exact-thread outgoing answers, differing subjects/questions, stale approvals, current incoming questions, uncertain receipts and in-flight preservation. The full release gate and production build are required before publishing. The local companion has 93 passing focused tests and a passing TypeScript check. Exact commit, deployment, live status and remaining provider gaps are recorded in the local versioned verification receipt. Provider acceptance is not proof of inbox delivery.
