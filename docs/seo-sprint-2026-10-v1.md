## What counts as a result

1. Confirmed external customer paid orders and their recorded gross, with internal tests excluded. Gross is not net revenue. Processor reconciliation and refunds are separate.
2. Google organic clicks, especially nonbranded eligible-product queries, with exact date windows, source and data lag.
3. Verified independent editorial links/referral traffic and observed AI citations/referrals. A drafted pitch, sister-site link or sampled AI answer is not proof of broad visibility.
4. Useful release evidence: tested code, live URL, commit, rationale and the metric expected to respond. Work volume is never a substitute for traffic or orders.

Traffic and sales improvements on a shared site are shared outcomes. Codex and Claude may claim authorship of verifiable changes, but cannot assign themselves causal sales credit without a sound experiment. Never change another agent's work just to make the site look different.

## Search and evidence rules

Use the existing reviewed publication policy. Do not promote held products by hiding chemical identity, changing certificates or removing safety-relevant facts. Keep scientific conclusions faithful to their studies; no fabricated efficacy, bioavailability, testing or testimonials. Preserve original lab records.

Use a fixed query cohort for progress: each eligible product's exact name + strength + research vial, plus how to read a peptide COA and research peptide supplier checklist. Freeze actual query strings in the first usable report; track branded queries separately. Search Console average position is an aggregate, not a guaranteed current rank. Manual search/AI samples require exact prompt, engine, date, country, device and observed source links; they do not represent all users. Never manufacture visits, clicks or conversations to inflate measurement.

Use legitimate editorial relationships and helpful original information. No purchased ranking links, mass directory submissions, spam comments, copied competitor pages, cloaking or bulk low-value pages. No spending/new external accounts/press distribution until the required specific authorization and terms steps are satisfied. Prepare concrete material first. Do not send messages to Claude or other chats without explicit human authorization; the shared log is available for them to read.
