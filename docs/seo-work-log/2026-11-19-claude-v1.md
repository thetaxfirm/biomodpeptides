## How this was measured

All 80 ranking queries were answered (78 with 10 results, 2 with 9). The same product query patterns as November 18 were used for all 35 sitemap products. Five more ranking queries than November 18 (80 against 75), so the "no Biomod result" totals are not directly comparable.

Pacing differed from earlier runs: the browser pane was hidden and its timers were slowed, so most queries went out in short bursts instead of 3 s apart. DuckDuckGo started returning empty pages after about 66 queries; the rest were sent 8 to 10 s apart and answered.

Indexed count: three `site:` variants answered ("site:trybiomod.com", "... product", "... research"). The fourth ("... compounds") was rate-limited twice and is not measured. The floor below is the union of those three and every trybiomod.com URL seen in ranking results.
