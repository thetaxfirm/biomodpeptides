## Change: spray titles now name the compound (shipped)

A crawl of all 83 sitemap pages found every product page with 26 or more inbound internal links, so linking is not what holds the unranked sprays back. Their titles were. A spray is sold under a mark, and the title carried the mark alone: "Buy APEX 60mg Spray". A search for the compound in spray form had nothing in the title to match, and the baseline above shows no trybiomod page for any of those searches.
