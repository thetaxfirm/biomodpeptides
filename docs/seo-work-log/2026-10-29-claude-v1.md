## Why this change

`site:biomodpeptides.com` lists 7 old-domain URLs. Two that rank for brand searches, /tag/peptides/ (#3 for "biomod peptides") and /peptide-softgel-starter-kit-beginners/ (#3 for "biomod softgels"), plus two other listed posts, still ended in a 404 on trybiomod.com after the domain move, so their link value was being dropped instead of passed to a live page.
