## Change: compound reference pages link their collections and each other (shipped)

A crawl of all 83 sitemap pages counted the internal links pointing at each one. After yesterday's change the least-linked pages were the 17 compound reference pages (3 to 6 links each, against 26 or more for a product page). Each reference page linked out only to its own products, so a crawler reaching "Semax" had no path to "Selank" or to the collection both belong to. These are the pages that carry the CAS, formula and sequence data for compound-name searches.

What changed:

| Page | Added |
| --- | --- |
| 14 compound reference pages | "Where <compound> is catalogued": the published collections holding one of its products, research areas first, then the spray collection |
| 12 of those pages | "Related compound references": the other compounds of the same research area (for example Semax lists Selank and DSIP) |
| /collections/growth-hormone-axis, /mitochondrial-research, /neuroscience-research | "Side-by-side comparisons": the comparison page whose two compounds are both in that collection |

Every link is derived from the catalog data, goes only to a page in the sitemap, and a page with nothing to link shows no section. Epitalon, Kisspeptin-10 and Thymosin Alpha-1 are in no published collection and are unchanged. The spray collection is listed as a place a compound is sold but does not make two compounds "related". No title, description, price, structured data or existing copy changed, and no new wording uses "peptide".
