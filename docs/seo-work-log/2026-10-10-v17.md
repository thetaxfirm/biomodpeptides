## Confirmed on-site search defect and correction

The actual matcher accepted the canonical names/identities and integer strengths for all 17 eligible products (34 checks), but rejected the equivalent trailing-zero decimal strengths for all 17. Before editing, the normal production Shop page independently showed zero results for SS31 50.0mg.

The tokenizer now removes trailing decimal zeros only from a pure decimal quantity token immediately followed by an already recognized mg, mcg, g or ml unit. This makes 50.0 mg equivalent to 50 mg. String operations preserve all nonzero digits without floating-point rounding. Units are not converted; molecular numbers, CAS/SKU tokens without quantity units, product form, blend totals and publication holds retain their existing rules. No catalog identity, size, price, availability or certificate is changed. This is a search-friction correction, not an explanation for Google ranking or a new product publication.

Affected files: lib/product-search.ts; tests/product-search-peptide-v1.cjs; tests/product-search-blend-strength-v1.cjs; this log. Affected storefront: /shop search, including SS31 50.0mg, BPC157 10.00mg and GSH1500.0mg.
