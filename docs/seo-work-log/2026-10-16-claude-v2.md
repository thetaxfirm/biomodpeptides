## Why this change

Product pages are still the missing part of the index (1 of 17). This morning's 628f8c0 made /faq, /about, /quality-standard and /choosing-a-research-supplier, four pages Bing has already indexed, link every vial. But the sitemap still dated those pages October 12-13, so Bing's sitemap reader had no reason to recrawl them and find the new links. `lib/sitemap-lastmod.ts` says each date must be bumped in the same commit as the content change; that step was missed. /shipping-policy and /contact also changed on October 14 (9595696 preorder wording, 1ef1cb4 vial lot field) and kept the October 12 date.
