- November 16 (69fc7cc): headings on all 14 spray and softgel pages.
- November 17 and 18 (ef23210, 615b6a1): product lists on the compound reference pages, and back-links from the product and testing pages.
- November 23 (3a22a06): the spray, softgel and lab-supply list on /shop, four guide pages and every vial page.

`lib/sitemap-lastmod.ts` follows the rule that a header or footer change dates every page, so the sitewide date is now November 24 and all 83 pages carry it. No page content, title, description, price or structured data changed. The date is a fixed release date, not the clock.

The lastmod test now reads the real header, footer and spray title builder: if the Sprays link or a compound-naming spray title is present and any page is dated earlier than November 24, the release gate fails.
