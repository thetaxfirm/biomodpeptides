## Change: the shop, the guides and the product pages now link every spray, softgel and lab supply (shipped)

Follow-up to carried gap 4 from November 21 (SOLARA, GLOW, EQUIL, CHISEL, DELTA and SCULPTOR do not rank for their own name). Their titles, headings, descriptions and markup are built the same way as the sprays and softgels that hold #1, so the pages themselves are not the difference. The difference found today is internal links.

Counted on the live site before the change, across all 83 sitemap pages:

- /shop linked 33 vials and none of the 14 sprays and softgels. It opens on the vial list, and its category buttons are buttons, not links, so a crawler never reaches the other lists from there.
- The home page and /faq also linked none of them.
- Softgel product pages had 5 to 7 linking pages each, sprays 6 to 10, /product/acetic-acid-solution-1 one (/testing). A typical vial page has more than 25.

Now a plain list, "Research sprays, softgels and lab supplies", links all 15 published non-vial products by the same text as each page's own heading ("APEX Spray 60 mg", "BIONIC Softgels 60 caps", "Acetic Acid Solution 1%"):

- under the product grid on /shop, when the vial list is showing (not repeated when a search or another category already shows those products);
- inside the existing "All research peptides and compounds" index on /faq, /about, /quality-standard, /choosing-a-research-supplier and every vial product page.

The list is built from the catalog with the same publication check as the vial index, so held, blocked and unpublished products stay out; HEAT products are vials and are not in it. No title, description, price or structured data changed. No dosing, human-use or efficacy wording. This also closes the long-carried gap that the acetic acid page had one inbound link.
