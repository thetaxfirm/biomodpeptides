Problem (SEOnaut ERROR_NOT_VALID_HEADINGS, 24 occurrences, reconciled by URL with a direct audit of all 32 sitemap pages): `/testing`, `/research-use-only`, `/shipping-policy` and `/returns-refunds` have one h1 and no h2, and the shared footer's column titles ("Shop & Account", "Research & Support", "Policies") were h3, so each page jumped from h1 to h3. Titles are 47 to 70 characters; the long-title findings are the shared " | Biomod Research Supply Company" suffix and were not changed.
