## Change: held-product certificate PDFs kept out of search

Problem: Bing indexed `/certificates/klow-80mg-...pdf` and `/certificates/run-and-gun-10mg-...pdf`. KLOW (`klow-80mg`) and Run & Gun (`run-gun-10mg`) are on specification holds in `lib/product-specification-review-v1.json`, and holds must stay noindex. Certificate PDFs are Cloudflare static assets, so the proxy's `X-Robots-Tag` never reached them (no proxy headers on the PDF responses).

Commit 50d717f, pushed to main as a fast-forward:
- `public/_headers` sends `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` for exactly those two PDFs. The files stay at the same URLs and remain readable for customer verification; no lab document was changed.
- `tests/certificate-hold-noindex-v1.cjs` (in the release gate) maps every PDF in `public/certificates` to its product through `lib/coas-reconciliations-v1.json`, applies the real hold list and the published release configuration, and fails if a held product's certificate is indexable or an indexable product's certificate is hidden. It failed against an empty `_headers`.

This closes the "needs an owner decision" item from Oct 29-30: noindex hides nothing from people, and the owner's hold rule already requires held products to stay out of search.
