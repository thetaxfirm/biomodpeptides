## Why this change

Bing still lists old biomodpeptides.com URLs for the brand. Checking every one it returned today (16 URLs across `site:biomodpeptides.com` and four related queries) showed six that still ended in a 404 at trybiomod.com after the domain 301:

- /tag/scientific-reproducibility/, /tag/reproducibility/, /tag/research-reproducibility/ (tag archives for articles on storage, purity and reagent quality)
- /tag/spray-formulation/ (spray stability articles)
- /tag/longevity-supplement-peptides/ (snippet is the old homepage tagline)
- /longevity-peptide-stack/ (old blend page; no blend is sold now)

A 404 at the end of a redirect throws away whatever ranking signal those URLs carry. The other ten resolve to live pages.
