## Changes

It now reuses the reviewed product matcher and matches whole public lot, report and SKU identifiers, retaining all digits and leading zeros. Combined queries must agree with the same product record. Existing product/lot/status filters remain conjunctive. Pending and mismatched documentation remain labeled; restricted certificate metadata is not searched. No certificate, product identity, inventory, price or publication status changed.

The SS-31 50 mg page had no source-assigned SKU but Product markup substituted internal record number 5027. Optional sku is now omitted when the source field is empty or whitespace. The 16 assigned SKUs and all 17 eligible products' stable Product IDs, Offers, original certificate properties and publication controls remain intact. No GTIN or MPN was invented. Relevant definitions: https://schema.org/sku and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/merchant-listing.
