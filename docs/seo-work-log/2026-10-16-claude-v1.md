## Why this change

The pages Bing has indexed are the homepage, /shop and five editorial pages. The homepage links to 17 products and /shop to 33. /faq, /about, /quality-standard, /choosing-a-research-supplier and /research-use-only linked to none, not even from the footer. Product pages are still the missing piece of the index, and links from pages Bing already trusts are the on-site way to get them crawled.
