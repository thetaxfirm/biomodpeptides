## Why this change

`lib/sitemap-lastmod.ts` says a sitewide change dates every page, but `siteLastmod` was still October 17. Since then the footer on every page changed twice: 710dc82 (October 20) added the softgel trademark line and d52581c (October 23) reworded it. So the sitemap told Bing nothing had changed for a week on the 12 product pages, /softgels, /news and the policy pages it has not indexed yet. Product pages were also dated October 19 though DSIP and Epitalon identities changed October 23. An accurate lastmod is the on-site recrawl signal Bing documents; this is not a "set everything to today" bump, since each page did change on October 23.
