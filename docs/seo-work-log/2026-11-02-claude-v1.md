## BPC-157 LCP profiling (no change made)

Three Lighthouse runs on `/product/bpc-157-10mg?measurement=off` (simulated mobile) gave performance scores of 0.89, 0.82 and 0.80, with LCP of 3.7 s, 4.4 s and 4.4 s and 634 KB transferred. The LCP element is the gallery SVG image. It loads in about 90 ms right after its preload; the reported 2.0 s "element render delay" is Lantern simulation, which counts every first-load script because the observed LCP equals the observed FCP (0.69 s, 2.5 s and 2.6 s in the traces). The three related-product images (about 79 KB) start after the LCP image and do not delay it, so lazy-loading them alone would not move LCP. The levers left are first-load JavaScript (the `experience` chunk at 107 KB and `shipping-terms` at 68 KB) and the 54 KB render-blocking `index` CSS.

Also found: `ProductImage` draws framed vials as SVG `<image>`, which ignores `loading="lazy"`. Every product thumbnail therefore loads eagerly: 33 on `/shop` and `/testing`, and 20 on the home page. A lazy path (an HTML `<img>` inside `foreignObject`, or a sized HTML frame) changes how product images render across the store, and Safari/iOS cannot be checked from here. It was not shipped unattended; it should ship only with a WebKit check.
