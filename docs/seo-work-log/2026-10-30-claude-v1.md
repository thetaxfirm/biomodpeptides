## Change: baseline action queue item 2 (BPC-157 LCP)

Profile first (Lighthouse 13.6.0, `?measurement=off`, default mobile simulation, three runs before the change):

- LCP element: the gallery SVG `<image href="/products/bpc-157-10mg-brand-v37.webp">` (21 KiB).
- Observed breakdown: TTFB 586 ms, load delay 10 ms, load 219 ms, render delay 30 ms. Render delay is now small.
- The image was requested at **Medium** priority alongside about 400 KiB of High-priority scripts. SVG `<image>` ignores `loading` and `fetchpriority`, so `ProductImage` could not hint it. Lighthouse flagged "fetchpriority=high should be applied". Related-product images (DSIP etc.) also load immediately for the same reason.

Commit 3da0bed, pushed to main as a fast-forward: eager `ProductImage` calls `react-dom` `preload(src, { as: 'image', fetchPriority: 'high' })`. Lazy list, cart and related images are unchanged. `tests/lcp-image-priority-v1.cjs` (renders the real BPC gallery and three lazy images; fails on the previous code) is in the release gate.
