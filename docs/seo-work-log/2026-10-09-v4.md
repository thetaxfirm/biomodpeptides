## Evidence and diagnosis

Google URL Inspection confirmed the homepage **already indexed**, last crawl October 9 at 12:02:01 AM (Search Console display), Googlebot smartphone. Crawl and indexing allowed; fetch successful. User-declared canonical: None. Google-selected canonical: inspected URL. The screenshot does not prove a site-wide absence or penalty. A separate web search for “biomod peptides trybiomod.com” returned TryBiomod About, Testing, FAQ and supplier-guide pages before this deployment; not a controlled Google rank report and not a gain attributed to this work.

Brand signals were ambiguous: homepage title ended only BIOMOD, WebSite/Organization/OG site names were BIOMOD with no aliases, and the visible introduction did not connect Biomod Peptides to TryBiomod.com.

Raw live HTML placed canonical, title, description and robots metadata after the head for ordinary browsers and Googlebot. Installed Vinext intentionally streams dynamic metadata in a hidden body container for those user agents. The same metadata was in the head for Google-InspectionTool and Bingbot. This is a concrete rendering gap; it does not prove the sole cause of the earlier Google canonical finding or ranking result.
