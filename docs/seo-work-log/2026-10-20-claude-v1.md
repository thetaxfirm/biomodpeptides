## Why this change

Product pages are entering the index at about two a day, and the pages that lead them there are the already indexed ones. /shop is indexed and ranks for the brand, and its grid links every product page, but its structured data described only the Organization, WebSite and a plain WebPage: nothing told search engines which product pages belong to the catalog. The product pages' own markup already points back at /shop through BreadcrumbList. Today's change closes the loop from the catalog side, in markup Bing documents support for (CollectionPage with an ItemList), so the indexed shop page states its 17 indexable product pages explicitly instead of leaving them to be inferred from links among 33 grid cards, 16 of which lead to noindex pages.

This is a discovery and relationship signal, not a ranking claim; it does not add visible text and makes no product, effect or use statement.
