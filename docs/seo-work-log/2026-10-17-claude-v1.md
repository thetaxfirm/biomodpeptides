## Why this change

Today's audit (docs/seo-audit-release-2026-10-17-v1.md) found that about one page request in six hung, which most likely kept crawlers away from product pages; e2089e2 fixed that on Workers Paid. Bing now needs a reason to come back. Three releases today changed what visitors see, but none bumped the sitemap dates:

- 92d01ce renamed the header menu item "Nasal & spray" to "Sprays" on every page (verified live on /faq).
- da848db added "Priority Mail is free on orders of $250 or more" and the 2 p.m. Pacific same-day wording to every product page (verified live on /product/bpc-157-10mg).
- 589ae41 moved product images, og:image and Product JSON-LD images to WebP.

The sitemap still dated pages October 12 to 16, so Bing's sitemap reader had no signal to recrawl the 16 unindexed product pages.

## Change

Commit 2ca7adc (`lib/sitemap-lastmod.ts`): siteLastmod and productLastmod set to 2026-10-17, and `lastmodFor` now returns the later of a page's own date and the sitewide date, so a sitewide header change can no longer leave a page with an older date. Every URL is dated 2026-10-17 because every page really changed today; the dates stay hand-set constants tied to commits.

`tests/sitemap-lastmod-v1.cjs`: fails if any sitemap URL predates 2026-10-17, if a page's own date pulls it below the sitewide date, or if the lastmod module computes dates from the clock. The old "not every URL shares one date" assertion was replaced by that clock check, since a sitewide change legitimately dates every page the same day. Verified the test fails on the previous module ("/ shows the October 17 header change but is dated 2026-10-14") and passes on the new one.
